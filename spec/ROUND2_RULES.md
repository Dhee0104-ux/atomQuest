# atomQUEST 2026 | Round 2: Prototype Submission Guidelines
**Organizer:** Atomberg Technologies ("Why not?")  
**Source Document:** `atomQUEST 2026 - Stage 2 details.pdf` (Retrieved 2026-10-25)  
**Submission Deadline:** 4 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST  

---

## Important Points
* Only teams shortlisted in Round 1 can take part in this round.
* Your prototype must be in the same track and area of exploration as your abstract. Submissions on any other topic will be disqualified.
* The focus of this round is on hardware prototypes. A software-only prototype will be considered only if the value it delivers is immense.
* There is no minimum setup expected. Use whatever helps your team deliver the most value.
* If anything has changed since your abstract, please mention it in the progress report.

---

## 1. Video Requirements
* **Duration:** Maximum 90 seconds
* **File Size:** Up to 50 MB
* **The video should cover:**
  1. Team details.
  2. The problem you have identified and how you are solving it.
  3. Progress of your prototype so far.
  4. A demonstration of the core function of your prototype.
  5. What is done, what is pending, and the status of your safety or fail-safe mechanism (where applicable).

---

## 2. Progress Report Requirements
* **Format:** PDF, 1-2 pages
* **The progress report should cover:**
  1. Team name, college, track and area of exploration.
  2. The problem you have identified and how you are solving it.
  3. Components or modules completed so far, including the status of anything flagged as a sourcing risk in your abstract.
  4. Amount spent so far against the cost estimate in your abstract.
  5. Changes since the abstract, if any: revised target claim, and any delay in the plan with the reason.
  6. Status of the safety or fail-safe mechanism (where applicable).
  7. Planned next steps for Round 3.
  * *Photos or screenshots of your prototype may be added within the report.*

---

## 3. Submission Guidelines
* Upload the video and the progress report directly on the competition platform, where you submitted your abstract.
* Only one video and one progress report per team will be considered. Please check that both files open properly before submitting.

---

## 4. Naming Convention (Strict Mandatory Format)
* **Video:** `atomQUEST2026_[TeamName]_[CollegeName]_Track[No]_Video` (e.g. `.mp4`)
* **Report:** `atomQUEST2026_[TeamName]_[CollegeName]_Track[No]_Report.pdf`
* **Example:** `atomQUEST2026_Aerominds_IITDelhi_Track1_Video`
* *Submissions that do not follow this format will not be considered for evaluation.*

---

## 5. Evaluation Criteria
* Teams will be evaluated on the value their solution delivers and the progress made so far.
