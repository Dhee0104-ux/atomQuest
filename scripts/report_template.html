1. Problem Identified & Technical Value Proposition

Modern connected water purifiers operate on blind, fixed calendar intervals (replacing filters every 6 or 12 months) or crude volume totalizers. This introduces catastrophic inefficiencies: households with clean municipal supplies (e.g. Cauvery, <200 ppm) discard cartridges with 35% residual membrane life, causing unnecessary consumer expenditure and plastic waste. Conversely, sudden upstream shocks (pipe bursts, municipal repairs, borewell salinity surges >1000 ppm) foul sediment pre-filters and scale RO membranes prematurely without warning, quietly exposing families to degraded water.

AquaMind provides a complete edge-to-cloud intelligence suite: an auditable, physics-grounded prognostic baseline, correlated with privacy-preserving neighborhood cluster intelligence (k-anonymity, N ≥ 10) to distinguish upstream municipal disturbances from local machine degradation, paired with closed-loop DIY maintenance verification.

5. Safety & Fail-Safe Mechanisms

Three automated fail-safe interlocks are active:

  • O-Ring & Bypass Interlock: Detects sudden pressure collapse or TDS spike post-replacement (<85% rejection), locking the dispense solenoid.
  • Mandatory Flush Lock: Flags Outcome 2: Pending Flush until ≥5.0L is flushed, preventing ingestion of loose carbon fines.
  • Cavitation Protection: Shuts off booster pump if inlet pressure drops <5 psi to prevent dry-run pump burnout.
